Rules of the Void

🧠 Board Growth & Enemies

You are the King . The further you go, the deeper you fall into the Void, expanding the battlefield:

  • Waves 1-4: 6x6 Board. Enemies: Pawns and Knights .
  • Waves 5-9: 7x7 Board. Rook joins the fray.
  • Waves 10-14: 8x8 Board. Bishop appears.
  • Waves 15+: 9x9 Board. Full army of the Void.

⚠️ Critical Rule: Pawn Promotion

If an enemy Pawn reaches the very bottom of the board, it will **promote to a Queen **! The Queen strikes in all directions with unlimited range. Don't let them pass!

🩸 Resources

Every single move costs 1 🩸 Blood (unless upgraded). Defeating an enemy restores your Blood. Tiles crumble into the Void 🕳️ behind you.
